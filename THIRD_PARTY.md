## Not third-party, despite appearances

- `system/datasets/**` and `system/incidentmind-data/**` are **synthetic** corpora
  generated by `system/datasets/generate_evidence.js`. The service names resemble
  a generic e-commerce estate and the personas (`alice`, `bob`, `charlie`) are
  fictional. No real organisation's data, logs, tickets or messages are included.
- The service names in `realworld/results/**` (`cartservice`, `checkoutservice`, …)
  are those of the Online Boutique benchmark; the **measurements** of those
  services are ours.
